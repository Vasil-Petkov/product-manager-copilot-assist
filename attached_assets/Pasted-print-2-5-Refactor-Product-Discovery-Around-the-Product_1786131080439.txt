print 2.5 – Refactor Product Discovery Around the Product Idea Domain Model

You are continuing development of Product Manager Copilot Assist.

IMPORTANT

Do NOT create new modules.

Do NOT redesign the UI.

Do NOT change the navigation.

Do NOT build Prioritization, Validation, Roadmap, Documentation or Analytics yet.

Continue improving only the Product Discovery module.

The objective of this sprint is to establish the application's core domain model before implementing additional functionality.

Core Architecture Decision

The application must be redesigned around a single business entity called Product Idea.

This is the most important architectural decision of the application.

A Product Idea is NOT simply a database record.

A Product Idea represents a business opportunity that evolves throughout the entire Product Management lifecycle.

Every future module must enrich the same Product Idea instead of creating new disconnected objects.

Everything in the application revolves around Product Ideas.

Continuous Improvement

The Product Idea remains the same object during its entire lifecycle.

Only its status and associated information change.

Refactor Existing Product Opportunity

The current "Opportunity" entity should become the Product Idea entity.

Rename database tables, API models and services where appropriate.

If renaming would introduce unnecessary migration complexity, keep the internal database name temporarily but expose the concept throughout the application as Product Idea.

Future modules must reference Product Ideas.

Product Idea Workspace

Instead of displaying only a list of Product Ideas, every Product Idea should have its own dedicated workspace.

Selecting a Product Idea should open a complete detail page.

The workspace should become the central place where every future module stores information.

Refactor the schema so Product Idea becomes the central entity.

All future modules must reference Product Ideas using relationships.

Avoid duplicated data.

Normalize the schema.

Use foreign keys.

Design for extensibility.

Do NOT implement future modules.

However, design Product Ideas so they can later be enriched by:

Final Goal

At the end of this sprint, Product Discovery should no longer be a collection of pages.

It should become a complete Product Idea Management Workspace.

Every Product Idea should represent the digital twin of a real product opportunity, continuously enriched by AI, evidence, customer feedback, meetings, stakeholders, competitors, and future modules.

The Product Idea is the heart of the entire application.

Build the architecture accordingly.
