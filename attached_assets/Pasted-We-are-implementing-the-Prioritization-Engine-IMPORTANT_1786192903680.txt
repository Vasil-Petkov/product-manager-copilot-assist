==================================================
1. RICE
==================================================

Formula

RICE = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort

Definitions

Reach
Number of customers expected to benefit within the selected period.

Impact

Massive = 3
High = 2
Medium = 1
Low = 0.5
Minimal = 0.25

Confidence

0–100%

Effort

Engineering effort measured in Story Points.

Display

• Individual values
• Final RICE Score
• AI explanation

==================================================
2. ICE
==================================================

Formula

ICE = Impact × Confidence × Ease

Definitions

Impact

1–10

Confidence

1–10

Ease

1–10

Display

• ICE Score
• AI explanation

==================================================
3. MoSCoW
==================================================

No mathematical formula.

Every Product Idea must be classified as

• Must Have
• Should Have
• Could Have
• Won't Have

AI should explain why the feature belongs in this category.

==================================================
4. Weighted Scoring
==================================================

Formula

Weighted Score =
(Customer Value × 0.25)
+
(Revenue Impact × 0.20)
+
(Strategic Alignment × 0.20)
+
(Technical Complexity × 0.15)
+
(Competitive Advantage × 0.10)
+
(Risk × 0.10)

Each criterion is scored from 1–10.

The weights must be configurable by administrators in the future.

Display

• Individual criterion scores
• Final Weighted Score
• AI explanation

==================================================
5. Value vs Effort
==================================================

This framework has no mathematical formula.

Business Value

1–10

Engineering Effort

1–10

Display the Product Idea on a 2x2 matrix:

• High Value / Low Effort
• High Value / High Effort
• Low Value / Low Effort
• Low Value / High Effort

AI should explain the recommendation.

==================================================
