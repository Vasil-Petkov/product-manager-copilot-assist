I want you to create a web site/app that help product managers called Product Manager Copilot Assist which help the product managers to be more effective and to save time on repetitive tasks.
Module 1 is product discovery where you support inputs from social media, internal idea portals, competitor intelligence, meeting transcripts, stakeholder feedback, and prepare for future sources like support desks, CRMs, app reviews, NPS, and analytics. Every input should go through an AI pipeline that cleans, deduplicates, clusters, detects sentiment, estimates impact and urgency, and produces a structured discovery opportunity. Each opportunity must include an evidence panel showing source counts and examples. Module 2 is prioritisation. Every discovery opportunity flows into prioritisation frameworks: Rice, Ice, Moscow, Kano, etcetera. Allow manual adjustments, but generate AI insights that explain rankings, key assumptions, missing data, risks, dependencies, and suggested release windows. The end-to-end workflow is Signals, AI normalisation, discovery opportunities, evidence panel, prioritisation, AI recommendations, roadmap candidates, and later PRD generation and Jira export. Non-functional use a modern SaaS UI with a clean architecture, extensible integration layer, and strong type safety.
Product Discovery Module (MVP)
Role
You are a Senior Staff Software Engineer, AI Architect, and UX Designer.
Build the Product Discovery Module for an AI-powered SaaS application called Product Manager Copilot Assist.
The goal of this module is to become the single source of truth for Product Discovery by collecting product opportunities from multiple sources, organizing them automatically using AI, and presenting Product Managers with actionable insights.
The module should be production-ready, modular, scalable, and easy to extend with additional integrations.
________________________________________
Objective
Product Managers receive ideas, complaints, feature requests, and market information from many different places.
Instead of checking multiple systems every day, they should have one centralized Product Discovery workspace where all product opportunities are collected, analyzed, and prepared for prioritization.
The Product Discovery module does not prioritize ideas. Its responsibility ends with discovering, organizing, and enriching opportunities. Prioritization will be handled by a separate module.
