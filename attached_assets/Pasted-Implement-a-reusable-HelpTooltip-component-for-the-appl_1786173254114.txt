2. Place a small Help / Info icon (CircleHelp or Info icon from lucide-react) immediately to the right of each page title.

Examples:

Discovery Dashboard    (i)
Product Ideas          (i)
Feedback Sources       (i)
Competitors            (i)
Meetings               (i)
Stakeholders           (i)

- readable spacing
