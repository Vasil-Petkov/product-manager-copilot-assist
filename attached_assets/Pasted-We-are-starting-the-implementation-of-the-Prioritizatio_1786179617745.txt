MODULE PURPOSE

The Prioritization module helps Product Managers decide WHAT SHOULD BE BUILT NEXT.

It automatically evaluates every Product Idea collected from the Product Discovery module using multiple prioritization techniques and combines the results into one AI recommendation.

The user should never enter the same data twice.

All Product Ideas must automatically come from Product Discovery.
